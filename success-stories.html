The Crisis Wasn't What We Thought It Was.

After careful reflection and consolidation of the Farm Truths conversations, the patterns became clear:

The problem wasn't 'labor shortage.' The problem was a broken co-ordination system.

What Farmers Told Us

"I need 50 workers tomorrow for harvesting. I have no idea if they'll show up."

"Contractors promise one wage, workers demand another when they arrive."

"My grapes are ready today. If workers don't come for 3 days, I lose 30% of my crop value."

"Good workers from last season? I have no way to find them again."

"Workers get no training, no insurance, no predictability. Why would they stay?"

What Workers Told Us

"We don't know where work is available until the morning."

"Wage negotiation happens on-site. It's always tense."

"If we get injured, there's no safety net."

"We want steady work, not day-to-day uncertainty."

This wasn't a supply-demand mismatch. This was a trust and coordination failure — invisible to policy, ignored by tech platforms, unsolved by traditional agriculture. ₹thousands lost per farmer per season. Tens of thousands of workers stuck in informal chaos. An entire system running on phone calls and word-of-mouth.

This was a category-defining opportunity hiding in plain sight.

We Didn't Just Find A Problem. We Built The Solution Blueprint Before The Founders Arrived.

Most accelerators say: "Bring us your idea, and we'll help you validate it." We flipped it.

We validated the opportunity first. Then we found the builders.

After extensive consultations and real user feedback from Farm Truths we had:

A Complete Venture Blueprint

  • Vision: A labor intelligence platform that formalizes farm hiring
  • Market sizing: Maharashtra grapes + bananas = substantial wage pool annually
  • Unit economics: Take-rate per worker-day + tool rental + embedded finance
  • Pilot blueprint: Nashik grape farms, 500 worker-days in first month
  • Risk map: Seasonality, churn, regulatory constraints — and mitigations

Pilot-Ready Access

The Guarantee
