WHICH BUILDER ARE YOU?

We care about what you've built, not your resume.

The Resilient Founder

Learn more

The Resilient Founder

You've built before. Now you're building better. You've started something, a company, a product, a team. It worked, or it didn't, but you learned. You know the difference between a pitch deck and a plan. You've tasted failure and chose to try again. You're not here to theorize about agriculture, you're here to execute. You might be a second-time founder pivoting into agriculture. Or someone who built a successful business elsewhere and wants to tackle a problem that matters.

The Tech Visionary

Learn more

The Tech Visionary

You can build anything. Now you'll build something that matters. You're an engineer, developer, data scientist, or hardware maker. You've built apps, algorithms, systems, or devices. You've worked in tech but are tired of solving trivial problems. You want your skills to matter. You're willing to get your hands dirty (literally) to understand agriculture from the ground up. You know technology is a tool, not a solution. You're ready to learn what problems are actually worth solving.

The Native Solutionist

Learn more

The Native Solutionist

You're a farmer, FPO operator, rural mechanic, supply chain insider, or agronomy consultant. You've seen the pain points firsthand. You know the workarounds farmers use because formal systems fail them. You understand the context that outsiders miss. You might not know how to code or pitch to investors. But you know the difference between what sounds good in a boardroom and what works in a field.
