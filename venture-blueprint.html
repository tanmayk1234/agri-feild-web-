Example Opportunity

First-Mile to Last-Mile Logistics

India's agricultural supply chain loses ₹92,000 crore annually to post-harvest waste. The problem isn't production—it's movement.

What's Broken

No cold chain at farm gate. Produce sits in open sun for 6-12 hours before first pickup.

Fragmented transport. 3-5 middlemen between farm and consumer. Each adds cost, delay, and quality loss.

No SLA-driven logistics. No guaranteed pickup times. No temperature tracking. No accountability.

25-40% post-harvest loss. Grapes, tomatoes, strawberries—perishables die before reaching markets.

Who Suffers

Small farmers (< 2 hectares) — can't afford own transport, dependent on traders.

FPOs — aggregate but can't move produce fast enough for perishables.

Export markets — India loses billions in export-grade produce to logistics failures.

Consumers — pay premium prices for produce that's already 5-7 days old.
