ABOUT THE VENTURE BLUEPRINT

The Venture Blueprint is the core methodology behind Agri Founders Field — India's only on-farm venture building program, a joint initiative by Sahyadri Farms, Digital Impact Square (DISQ), and Maharashtra State Innovation Society (MSIS).

We don't ask founders to guess what to build. Instead, we invest months in deep field research — interviewing 1,000+ stakeholders, mapping value chain frictions, and sizing real market opportunities — so every builder starts with a validated problem and guaranteed pilot customers.

Each blueprint is backed by real data: market sizing, unit economics, competitive analysis, and risk frameworks. Builders get hands-on access to Sahyadri's 15,000+ farmer network, on-farm R&D facilities, and practitioner mentors who have built ₹2,000+ crore agri-ventures.

HOW WE FIND OPPORTUNITIES WORTH SOLVING

Before we ask you to build, we do the hard work:

Step 01

Deep Field Research

We interview 1,000+ stakeholders across the value chain — farmers, FPO operators, packhouse managers, transporters, wholesalers, retailers.

Step 02

Friction Mapping

We identify where value is lost: post-harvest losses, broken logistics, information asymmetry, financing gaps.

Step 03

Market Sizing

We quantify the total addressable market, validate willingness to pay, and estimate unit economics.

Step 04

Solution Validation

We test assumptions with pilot customers and secure early LOIs (Letters of Intent) before you even start building.

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR: BUILDERS WHO CAN SOLVE THIS

Build an asset-light, SLA-driven logistics network that:

Solves Empty Returns

Backhaul engine pairing corridors (e.g., produce Nashik→Mumbai, packaging Mumbai→Nashik) = 30-50% reduction in empty kilometers

Creates Embedded Revenue

Subscription model + fuel/toll payments + cargo insurance + invoice factoring

Reduces Shrinkage

Proper handling SOPs + predictable delivery windows = 40% reduction in quality losses

Improves Reliability

Control tower + GPS tracking + proof-of-delivery = on-time delivery >85%

Why This Works:

  • Start with 1-2 high-value corridors (Nashik-Mumbai, Punjab-Delhi)
  • Partner with existing fleets (asset-light, not asset-heavy)
  • Layer SLA premium + recurring revenue (not just freight brokerage)
  • Anchor demand from organized retail, exporters, food services
